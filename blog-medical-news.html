Vital Care opens a new digital imaging wing

Faster scans, shorter waits, and clearer results for patients.

VC
Vital Care Team
3 min read May 20, 2026
Share

Vital Care's new digital imaging wing opens this month, bringing X-ray, ultrasound, CT, and MRI services together under one roof with newer equipment and a redesigned patient flow. Here's what's changing, and what it means the next time you need a scan.

40%Shorter average wait for routine scans
24–48hTypical result turnaround, down from a week
4Imaging modalities under one roof
What's new

Newer machines, sharper images

The wing adds a new MRI suite and a low-dose CT scanner, alongside upgraded ultrasound and X-ray equipment. In practice, that means clearer images on the first attempt, fewer repeat scans, and lower radiation exposure where CT is involved.

What's new

Shorter waits, start to finish

Booking, scanning, and results have been redesigned as one connected process rather than three separate stops. Referrals route directly into imaging scheduling, and most routine scans that once took two to three weeks to book are now available within days.

"A scan is only useful if it leads somewhere quickly. Most of what we've rebuilt here is about closing the gap between 'you need imaging' and 'here's what we found.'"
What's new

Results you can actually see

Patients now get secure online access to their images and reports, rather than waiting on a callback. Referring providers see results the moment they're finalized too, which shortens the loop between a scan and whatever comes next — a treatment plan, a specialist referral, or the all-clear.

A note from the clinic

The imaging wing is open now for both walk-in referrals and scheduled appointments. If you already have a scan booked elsewhere in the building, no action is needed — your appointment has automatically moved to the new suite.

The goal behind the new wing is simple: make imaging feel like one smooth step in your care rather than a separate errand with its own delays. We'll keep sharing updates as the space settles into its first few months.
